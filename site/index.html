Set it up in about 30 minutes

You need a Mac with Xcode, your iPhone and its cable, and the ring's charger. A free Apple ID works.

Read the full guide

  1. Install the app from your Mac

    Connect the iPhone and tap Trust. Turn on Developer Mode: the switch shows after the Mac connects once. Run the script, and keep the iPhone unlocked until the app opens.

    git clone https://github.com/KenWuqianghao/open_health.git && cd open_health && ./apps/ios/install.sh

    Then trust your Apple ID in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. With a free Apple ID, run the script again every 7 days.

  2. Reset the ring on its charger, then pair

    Sync the official app one last time, delete it, and forget the ring in Bluetooth. Put the ring on the powered dock for 2 seconds, then turn the dock over and back until each light shows: blue, red, purple, yellow.

    Yellow starts the reset. Blinking blue means it is done. Do not set the ring up in the official app again. In Open Oura, tap Scan for Rings, then Pair.

  3. Optional: run your hub and scan to connect

    Run one script on your server, then scan the QR code on the hub's Connect page.

    git clone https://github.com/KenWuqianghao/oura-hub.git && cd oura-hub && ./deploy/install.sh

Before you reset your ring

Can I still use the official Oura app?

Not at the same time. A ring holds one key, so the reset removes Oura's key. To go back, reset the ring again and set it up in the official app. Sync the official app one last time before you switch.

Is anything sent to Oura or to you?

No. The app talks to the ring over Bluetooth and keeps everything on the iPhone. It sends data to one place only: a hub you run, after you connect it.

Which rings and phones work?

Oura Ring 3 (Gen3), Ring 4, and Ring 5, which use the same protocol. Pairing and sync were tested on a Ring 3 Horizon and a Ring 5. The app is iPhone only, iOS 17 or newer.

Why do I build it myself?

It is not on the App Store. The install script builds it with Xcode and your Apple ID. With a free Apple ID the app runs for 7 days per install; run the script again to renew it. Your data stays on the phone.

Can I set it up on someone else's iPhone?

Yes. Connect their iPhone to your Mac and run the same script. Your Apple ID signs the app, so they trust it once in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. With a free Apple ID, connect their iPhone and run the script again every 7 days.

Are sleep stages included?

Oura's sleep model is not included. It is Oura's property, with cardiovascular age and workout detection. If you have the official app on your own phone, the guide shows how to add these models, following Thomas Marchand's work in open_health. Without them, the app shows the stages that the ring computes itself (confirmed on a Ring 3), and finds workouts with a simple activity rule. Everything else is the same: heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, temperature, the scores, stress, and the journal.

Are stress, resilience, and the cycle the same as Oura's?

No. Oura computes those in its cloud. Open Oura has its own open estimates from the same ring signals, and says so in the app.

Is this made by Oura?

No. Open Oura is an independent open-source project. It is not made, supported, or approved by Oura.

Built on Thomas Marchand's work

open_oura by Thomas Marchand (@thomasmc) reverse-engineered the ring's Bluetooth protocol, its authentication, and its event format, and ported the metric algorithms. Open Oura builds the iPhone app, the hub, and the setup on top of it.
